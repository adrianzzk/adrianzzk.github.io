## Brain Injury Associated with Chronic Kidney Disease

I investigate associations between chronic kidney disease and changes in brain structure and function, using medical imaging and data-driven methods to study disease mechanisms and assess risk.

## Adverse Effects Following Carbon-Ion Radiotherapy

I study normal-tissue injury and treatment-related adverse effects following carbon-ion radiotherapy. By integrating dosimetric parameters, linear energy transfer (LET), and medical imaging, I develop individualized risk-prediction methods.

## Skill Development for AI Agents

I design, develop, and deploy reusable skills for AI agents in research and real-world applications, focusing on workflow design, tool integration, and reliable task execution.
