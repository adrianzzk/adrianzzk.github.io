## Brain Injury Associated with Chronic Kidney Disease

I study the links between chronic kidney disease and alterations in brain structure and function, with an emphasis on medical imaging and data-driven methods for mechanistic investigation and risk assessment.

## Adverse Effects Following Carbon-Ion Radiotherapy

I investigate normal-tissue injury and adverse effects after carbon-ion radiotherapy. My work integrates dosimetric, linear energy transfer, and medical imaging information to develop individualized prediction methods.

## AI Agent Skill Development

I design, develop, and deploy reusable skills for AI agents, with a focus on research workflows, tool integration, and dependable task execution.

## Methods and Skills

Python, multimodal medical image analysis, deep learning, machine learning, data preprocessing, feature extraction, and AI agent skill development.
