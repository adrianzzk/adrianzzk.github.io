I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at Beijing Friendship Hospital, Capital Medical University. My work lies at the intersection of medical imaging, artificial intelligence, and clinically motivated research.

## Research Interests

- Brain injury associated with chronic kidney disease
- Prediction of adverse effects following carbon-ion radiotherapy
- AI agent skill development

My research combines multimodal medical data, medical image analysis, and machine learning to provide quantitative evidence for studying disease mechanisms and developing individualized risk prediction models.
