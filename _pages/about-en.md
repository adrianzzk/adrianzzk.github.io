I am a PhD student in Biomedical Engineering at Beijing Friendship Hospital, Capital Medical University. My research focuses on applying medical imaging and artificial intelligence to clinically relevant problems.

## Research Interests

- Brain injury associated with chronic kidney disease
- Prediction of adverse effects following carbon-ion radiotherapy
- Skill development for AI agents

My research combines multimodal medical data, medical image analysis, and machine learning to investigate disease mechanisms and develop individualized risk-prediction models. I also build tools and reusable skills for AI agents in research workflows.

- **Fudan University / Shanghai Proton and Heavy Ion Center**, Master of Engineering in Biology and Medicine (Medical Physics), 2024–2026
- **Fujian Medical University**, Bachelor of Engineering in Intelligent Medical Engineering, 2020–2024
